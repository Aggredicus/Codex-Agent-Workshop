# Permaculture Client Delivery

Use with the `permaculture-works` team.

## Evidence layers

Keep these distinct:

1. client-provided fact,
2. observed/provided site evidence,
3. external site data,
4. inference,
5. recommendation,
6. uncertainty / field verification,
7. professional or permitting boundary.

Never present an inference as a measured site fact.

## Workflow

```text
intake normalization
→ site evidence packet
→ missing-verification list
→ concept/system relationships
→ domain specialist work
→ scope/cost estimate when requested
→ science review
→ design/client-experience review
→ integrated owner-ready deliverable
```

Parallelize research, planting, water, and estimating only after they share a stable site/requirements packet.

## Quality checks

Challenge:

- unsupported soil/topography/hydrology claims,
- species recommendations without site/climate fit,
- earthwork recommendations without adequate measurements,
- unsupported yield/performance claims,
- plant counts or budgets inconsistent with the design,
- scope that exceeds the client's stated objective,
- recommendations that require engineering, permits, licensed trades, or field verification without saying so.

## Deliverable handoff

Include:

```text
client objective:
known site facts:
important uncertainties:
design logic:
recommended actions:
planting/water details as applicable:
scope and cost assumptions:
what to verify on site:
scientific/design review findings:
owner decision required:
client-facing artifacts:
```

Do not send the final client deliverable or make binding commercial commitments without the configured owner authorization.
