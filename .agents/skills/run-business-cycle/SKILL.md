# Run Business Cycle

Use this Skill for a real business outcome rather than a purely technical repository task.

## Establish the work packet

Record:

```text
business outcome:
deadline or urgency:
inputs:
required artifact/result:
acceptance criteria:
known constraints:
risk:
external consequence, if any:
human approval required for:
```

If the objective is broad, plan the smallest outcome that creates real business progress.

## Cycle

```text
understand
→ collect only necessary evidence
→ produce the result
→ verify/review it
→ package the owner-ready action
→ identify the next highest-value step
```

Use `caw plan` when multiple roles are useful.

## Common objectives

- identify and qualify leads,
- prepare outreach drafts and follow-up packets,
- turn intake into a client deliverable,
- prepare a proposal and estimate,
- plan a marketing campaign,
- analyze receivables/cash-flow data,
- improve ecommerce merchandising,
- document or automate an operating procedure,
- prepare a meeting/decision packet.

## Consequence boundary

Preparation may continue autonomously where safe. Stop for owner authorization before actions such as:

- sending external messages in the owner's name,
- committing the business to contractual terms,
- moving money or initiating payments,
- submitting tax/legal/regulatory filings,
- exposing or changing live credentials,
- destructive production/data changes,
- disclosing sensitive customer information,
- making professional/safety-critical claims outside the evidence and applicable scope.

## Completion

A business task is human-ready when the owner can understand what was done, inspect the evidence, and take or authorize the next action without reconstructing the agent conversation.
