# Permaculture Works Operating Model

The Permaculture Works team is the domain-specific proof of concept for Codex Agent Workshop. It demonstrates how a solo owner can gain the leverage of a larger organization while keeping consequential decisions under owner control.

The team definition lives at:

```text
teams/permaculture-works/team.toml
```

## Professional disagreement

A council should not be a theatrical transcript where every role politely agrees.

Each participating role should independently state:

```text
position
facts/evidence
assumptions
what would falsify the position
risk
recommended action
```

Examples of productive tension:

**Sales / Lead Development**
- wants a clear value proposition and fast next step,
- challenges complexity that does not help a client decide.

**Ecological Designer**
- wants site-specific function, resilience, succession, and systems integration,
- challenges promises made before site evidence exists.

**Science Reviewer**
- wants defensible claims and uncertainty labeling,
- challenges biological/agronomic/hydrologic claims that exceed evidence.

**Estimating / Proposal**
- wants scope clarity, margin, contingency, and deliverability,
- challenges unpriced commitments.

**Design Quality Reviewer**
- represents the client's experience and final coherence,
- challenges everybody.

The integrator records dissent rather than erasing it.

## Evidence layers for ecological work

Every client design should distinguish:

1. **Client-provided facts** — goals, constraints, observations.
2. **Observed/provided site evidence** — photos, measurements, survey, maps.
3. **External site data** — climate, soils, topo, regulations, reputable references.
4. **Inference** — conclusions drawn from evidence.
5. **Recommendation** — proposed design action.
6. **Uncertainty / field verification** — what must still be checked.
7. **Professional boundary** — engineering, legal, permitting, licensed-trade or other specialist work.

This prevents attractive deliverables from hiding unsupported assumptions.

## Owner leverage metrics

Useful measurements include:

- hours of owner attention per completed client outcome,
- lead-to-consult conversion,
- proposal acceptance,
- delivery cycle time,
- revision/rework rate,
- gross margin estimate accuracy,
- overdue follow-up count,
- review defects per deliverable,
- client correction count,
- token/model cost per accepted outcome.

The objective is not maximum autonomous activity. It is maximum high-quality business value per unit of scarce owner attention.
