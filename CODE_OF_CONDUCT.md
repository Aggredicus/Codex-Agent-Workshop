# Community Code of Conduct

This repository welcomes open-source contributors with varied backgrounds, abilities and levels of experience, including humans collaborating with AI agents.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, constructive and patient with volunteers.
- Critique code and evidence, not identities or personal characteristics.
- Assume a good-faith misunderstanding first; ask for clarification.
- Follow maintainer boundaries and do not pressure anyone to trade time or accept work.
- Do not post private information, credentials, client details, non-public source code, or third-party materials without permission.
- Keep task discussion and reviews focused on publicly verifiable software work.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, threats and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation
Use GitHub's **Report content** and **Block user** features for harassment or abuse. For unsafe source or security disclosures, use the private path described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than a public issue. Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate comments, close abusive issues or restrict participation under GitHub's moderation and permission controls. When in doubt, do not post sensitive personal details publicly.

This is community conduct guidance, not a contract requiring volunteer work.
